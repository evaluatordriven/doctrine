## What this is

**E-DI** helps you hand work to a person or an AI and still get good results.

You do **not** accept the first draft as final for important work.  
You say what “done” means **before** the work starts.  
Someone (or something) **checks** the work against that standard.  
You **fix** what failed, or you **stop**, by clear rules — not by vibes.  
You keep the final call when it matters.

This page only gets you moving.  
Full clarity (why, seats, success): **`EDI-PLAYBOOK.md`**.

## Human path (Delegator)

1. Skim **Playbook** §§1–2 and §6 (why + seats).  
2. Open **Operating instrument**.  
3. Choose `full` or `mvi` before filling. `mvi` fills the short list on the Instrument. `full` fills every line above the line. A blank required field on the chosen profile means you are not ready.  
4. Fill every field the chosen profile requires **before** anyone makes the work.  
   - Blank required field → you are not ready. That is the form working.  
5. Choose **Entry A** (work not made yet) or **Entry B** (work already exists).  
6. Make sure an **Evaluator** and a standard exist **before** the Maker builds (Entry A) or before you score existing work (Entry B). The Evaluator is not the Owner.  
7. Run the loop: make → check → fix or stop.  
8. End in one exit: **accepted**, **needs_owner**, or **rejected**.  
9. Keep a short record (see Example).

If stuck on values, risk, or “what good means,” that is an **Owner** question — not something to invent quietly.
